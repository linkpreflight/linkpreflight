MCP

Remote MCP server (Streamable HTTP): https://linkpreflight.dev/mcp. Tool: preflight(url).

{"mcpServers":{"linkpreflight":{"url":"https://linkpreflight.dev/mcp"}}}

What you get

  • HTTP status, full redirect chain, final URL
  • Dead link (DNS, refused, timeout, 404/410/5xx) and parked-domain detection
  • Content-Type and Content-Length
  • Whether /llms.txt exists
  • Phishing listing (OpenPhish public feed) and risk heuristics

No page content is extracted or stored. Private and internal addresses are refused.
